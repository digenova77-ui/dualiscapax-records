2. No evidence-grade inflation. Believed, Inspected, Tested, Proven remain distinct; a test applies only to its exact source, environment, suite, and execution record.

4. Trinity remains the sole authorization gate wherever governing instruments establish it. Resources, merit, fuel, stake, majority, signatures, or successful tests do not independently authorize.
5. A missing rung remains missing. Use UNKNOWN, NOT FOUND IN INSPECTED SCOPE, NOT RUN, or NOT ESTABLISHED.
6. No repository is merged, retired, archived, or deemed redundant solely because its name, scaffold, or root tree resembles another.
7. Preserve lawful exit, voluntary participation, independent histories, and continuity through dependency loss.
8. Vision is not reduced to implementation limits; vision is not reported as implemented capability.

## Four relationships that must never collapse

- **Storage location:** where bytes are held or mirrored.
- **Instrument authority:** which ratification, adoption, or founder act gives a rule standing.
- **Implementation location:** which repository contains code or a proposed expression.
- **Evidence location:** where tests, receipts, manifests, audits, and independent examinations are held.

A README, repository description, path, branch, or commit does not by itself settle constitutional authority.

## Three seats, three rings, two pipelines

**Three seats:** each seat repository states histories are not merged and no canon has been selected. A common index can point between seats; it cannot merge histories or select a canon.

**Three rings:** historical Rings vocabulary names DCLM, Iris, Twain, associated with logic, truth, pragmatism. Preserve this lineage and terminology.

**Two pipelines / dual capacity:** Founder’s later architecture describes two independent pipelines, each with its own internal dual capacity and tree/history, connected through a relationship/bridge. Two pipelines are not simply two rings; three rings do not prove two pipelines. Exact topology, five-layer mapping, and DAG/DAG-of-DAGs remain subject to source recovery and formal specification.

**Neck / relationship regulator (conceptual proposal):** (1) **Separate:** two identities/histories, no active bond. (2) **Bonded:** two identities remain distinct while one governed connection carries exchange and may create +1 emergent capacity. (3) **Merged:** one resulting entity/continuity, with original identities’ status and recoverability specified. Balanced does not mean identical capabilities, equal votes, or forced averaging; neither participant may silently dominate, erase, or inherit the other’s verdict. This is conceptual architecture, not established physical law or verified implementation.

Candidate transitions (PROPOSED): Separate→Bonded requires authenticated identities, voluntary consent, defined scope, protocol compatibility, lawful basis. Bonded→Separate may follow withdrawal, expiry, safety trigger, dependency failure, or lawful refusal; preserve histories and termination record. Bonded→Merged requires explicit, informed, mutual authorization and disclosure of reversibility, lineage, consequences. Merged→Separate only if decomposition/provenance recovery was designed and tested before merger; otherwise disclose irreversibility before consent. Malformed, ambiguous, unauthenticated, or unauthorized transitions fail closed. Fault is not a fourth relationship state.

## Authority, voting, +1, bounded experiments

Founder distinguishes a proposal’s right to be tested from a claim that it is true. A proposal may proceed to a bounded experiment under majority rule and support from at least one historically trusted constituent, including non-unanimous 2–1. +1 is cooperative/emergent contribution, not automatically a vote erasing dissent.

This is a **proposed experimental rule**, not an amendment to ratified Trinity law. Before implementation define electorate/standing, quorum, denominator, abstentions, threshold, tie and 1–2 semantics; define historical trust as declared/scoped/reviewable; eligible experiment classes; protect rights, consent, safety invariants, and independent lawful veto; specify scope, duration, resources, measurable hypothesis, success/failure criteria, stop conditions, rollback, independent observation; separate experiment permission from acceptance, adoption, production authorization, settlement.

A tie does not manufacture a winner. “Tie goes to Trinity” means Trinity applies governing law; unresolved authorization means HOLD/DENY, not invented casting vote. Exact policy needs the controlling instrument.
