## 2. The bloodstream — circulatory standards (PROPOSED)

**BOS-C1 — What flows.** The bloodstream carries four payload classes, and nothing else:
1. **Claims** — assertions travelling to be examined.
2. **Evidence** — receipts, digests, records; the oxygen of the system: a claim without evidence is a cell without oxygen, and it dies at the first valve.
3. **Authority** — permissions and authorizations; these flow only downhill from Trinity and are never manufactured in transit (no borrowed authority).
4. **Fuel** — compute and funding; capacity only, never authority (funding is fuel; it buys flow, never verdicts).

**BOS-C2 — The plasma.** The plasma is the carrying medium in which all four classes travel: the signed message envelope (the DCCP wrapper discipline: canonical bytes, provenance bound, digest-checkable). A payload outside the plasma is not in circulation — it is a rumour. If it isn't enveloped, signed, and hashable, it does not move.

**BOS-C3 — Every flow is five-tuple or it is not a flow.** Source · payload · provenance · destination · receipt. A movement missing any element is haemorrhage (see BOS-C6).

**BOS-C4 — Valves are gates.** Wherever flow changes pressure — where a claim becomes a decision, where evidence becomes standing, where fuel becomes work — there is a valve, and the valve is a gate under law: it opens on conditions in force, or it does not open. The narrowing is the heart's contraction: it is what makes flow one-directional toward verdict.

**BOS-C5 — Return path.** Circulation is a loop, not a drain. Every organ that receives must return: verdicts return to the record, residue returns to excretion, results return to the proposer. A flow with no return path is a bleed, even when it is authorised.

**BOS-C6 — Haemorrhage.** Any transfer of custody, value, or authority outside the ledger is haemorrhage, whatever its intention (Share Trust already states this law for custody; this standard extends it to all four payload classes).

**BOS-C7 — Blockage is lawful.** A HOLD is a clamp, not a failure: flow stops at the clamp, pressure is recorded, nothing routes around it. Routing around a clamp is the circulatory form of a gate bypass and is graded as a breach, not as initiative.

## 3. The digestive system — intake standards (PROPOSED)

**BOS-D1 — Intake.** The world enters raw: messages, files, prices, reports, claims by other minds. Raw intake is never absorbed directly. Anything absorbed unexamined is contamination, however friendly its source.

**BOS-D2 — Breakdown.** Intake is broken down by examination: point-counterpoint between seats, recomputation from primary sources, the four-part standard (no borrowed authority, identity, evidence, atomicity). Breakdown is mechanical where possible; judgement is reserved for what mechanics cannot settle.

**BOS-D3 — Absorption.** What survives breakdown and is receipted may be absorbed: into memory (standing fact), into law (adopted doctrine), or into work (a commissioned act). Absorption is graded: REPORTED is chewed, VERIFIED is digested, only the witnessed becomes tissue.

**BOS-D4 — Nutrients defined.** A nutrient is a unit of intake that an organ can metabolise into work or standing without residue above threshold. Concretely, tonight's estate already runs on five: verified facts · witnessed evidence · earned merit · lawful authority · fuel. The sustenance readings (reserves, balances, deadlines) are this system's blood panel: they measure nutrient levels, and starvation is a grade, not a surprise.

**BOS-D5 — Excretion.** What cannot be absorbed is residue, and residue is expelled by the Diamond protocol's fifth step, not stored: rejected findings carry a memory (so they are not re-ingested), and the invariants are never amended to accommodate what could not be digested.

**BOS-D6 — Ghosts are retained residue.** A corpus that reads pure while still carrying undigested material (David's Gemini notebook: the human-governance triad, refused a hundred times, still latent) is septic by this standard: surface purity with residue in the tissue. The test for digestion is not whether the surface reads clean, but whether the expelled material stays expelled under provocation.

## 4. The electric bloodstream — energy standard (PROPOSED)

**BOS-E1 — Charge is metered, never owned.** The bloodstream is electric: what flows carries charge, and charge is compute. The meter of that charge (eFuse in the current estate law) measures flow; it is never itself a nutrient, an asset, or a verdict. A system that starts eating its own meter has confused the gauge for the fuel.

**BOS-E2 — Voltage variance is a known impurity.** Identical code on slightly different voltage is not identical machinery (Founder's observation, Nov 12). Flow standards therefore grade the medium as well as the message: provenance includes the conditions of travel, not only the endpoints.

## 5. What this draft does NOT define (frontier, by name)

- **The nervous system** — signal speed, reflex arcs, what may act without reaching the chamber (the local Trinity inside the worker is a candidate; unexamined here).
- **The immune system** — canaries, refusal shapes, and ghost-detection as one system; the difference between infection (foreign claim), allergy (refusing the lawful), and auto-immune error (the system narrowing against itself).
- **Respiration** — how the organism exchanges with the outside at all: what is breath in, what is breath out, and who may hold it.
- **Flow rates** — every standard above is qualitative; no rate, pressure, or threshold in this draft is measured. Quantification is a separate, later act, and it will be measured or it will be UNKNOWN.
