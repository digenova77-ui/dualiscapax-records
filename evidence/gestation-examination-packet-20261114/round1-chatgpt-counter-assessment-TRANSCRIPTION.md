### D. Five overlooked developmental dependencies

2. **Failure containment** — if one organ fails, can it bring down the others? A compromised voice must not compromise the law; a failed perception engine must not corrupt the record.
3. **Recovery without permission from the original host** — can recovery occur without requiring the original machine to approve its own replacement? A major sovereignty question.
4. **Resource exhaustion** — what happens when the organism runs out of Fuel, storage, compute or connectivity? It must fail safely without inventing resources or bypassing authorization.
5. **Independent observability** — can an outside examiner establish what happened without trusting the agent that performed the operation? Essential for the entire verification philosophy.
