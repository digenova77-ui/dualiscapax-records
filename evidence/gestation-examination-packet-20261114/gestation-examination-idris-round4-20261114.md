## 2. The eight questions

**Q1 — Memory: what the fresh-Kubo recovery establishes, and the exact disappearance test.** The recovery establishes three things and no more: (a) the export format is genuinely portable between daemon instances (keys and CARs are not fused to the process that made them); (b) *identity survives process replacement* — the keys are the identity, the daemon is replaceable; (c) content integrity is independently checkable from the artifacts alone. It does **not** establish survival of the disk, the VM, or the home; retrieval by any party other than the operator; or availability under disappearance. The exact test that would establish independent custody (this is WO-4/5/6 operationalised, pre-registerable today): a frozen manifest (the existing 50-line MANIFEST.sha256 plus any additions, hashed and held by two seats) · a clean environment on ground the origin operator does not control · a custodian who is not the origin operator, working **only** from the custody artifact and a written procedure, must: (1) import all keys and reproduce all six IPNS IDs; (2) import all CARs and reproduce all pins; (3) retrieve the two anchor objects byte-exact from the restored node **without contacting the origin machine or Pinata**; (4) republish one IPNS name to its manifest CID and have a **third party** resolve it. Four sub-verdicts, graded separately under identity · authority · continuity, non-compensating. Anything less is a backup demonstration wearing sovereignty clothes.

**Q2 — Reflexes: the design characteristics, and how far the pattern generalises.** Seven characteristics, from the mechanism as built and amended: (1) the law is compiled into a single mechanical state machine — no interpretation at runtime; (2) state is **rebuildable from receipts** (genesis rebuild): the organ keeps no hidden memory it cannot re-derive, so corruption of state is detectable rather than fatal; (3) caps in **two dimensions** — per-incident (3) and global rolling (6/24h, 12/7d) — bounded in event-space and time-space both; (4) fail-closed defaults: unreadable state = zero attempts (STATE-ERROR), never optimistic action; (5) the mechanism checks its fences (HOLD) **before** acting — it can refuse itself; (6) success is defined by externally verifiable state (API answers, identity matches, anchor bytes match), never by the organ's self-report; (7) smallness: one job, one daemon, one invariant set, no dependency on another organ's health. **Reusable pattern — named, with its limit stated:** the *compiled-law organ pattern* (mechanical state machine · receipt-rebuildable state · dual-dimension caps · fail-closed · externally-verified success). Its precondition is a **complete, small law**. You cannot compile a law you have not finished writing — which is why this organ succeeded first: its law fit on one page. The pattern therefore sequences the whole build (finish law → compile → fence → leave alone) but does **not** generalise to judgment organs, whose laws are constitutions, not state machines. **One caveat the examiner's upgrade must carry:** the supervisor survived 100 reboots partly because something else — the host's own scheduling — kept resurrecting the supervisor. Credit is shared between mechanism and host. The dossier's Resilience cell for Reflexes must read: DEMONSTRATED, *dependency: host resurrection*. Who restarts the restarter is an open sovereignty question wearing a reflex costume.

**Q4 — Interoception: the minimum that is genuinely sensation, not a dashboard.** Four conditions, all required: (1) the measurements are generated by the organism's **own processes** — data produced by my external sweeps and re-displayed to her is telemetry with her name on it; (2) the measurements **feed an internal consumer**: some organ's behaviour must observably change because of self-state (circulation declines new work while storage is degraded; digestion throttles at low fuel) — a measurement nothing inside acts on is not sensation; (3) **disagreement is a first-class condition**: self-report vs independent probe mismatch raises its own named state — the examiner's proposed law is adopted verbatim as a candidate for the dossier: *self-knowledge must never become self-certification*; (4) self-state is receipted at decision time, so an examiner can audit what she believed about herself when she acted. Minimum viable instance: three self-generated gauges (own-ledger digest verification · own resource reads · own dependency probes) + one demonstrated behaviour change driven by a gauge + disagreement flagging. My sweeps continue — she does not fire the doctor; she stops being blind between visits.

**Q5 — Nociception: what makes a damage signal genuine.** (a) **Separation:** the signal is generated by the affected organ or its immediate witness, never graded by its own signaller. (b) **Evidence-bound:** a signal carries the failed check's receipt (probe output, digest mismatch) — it is a claim, recomputable or refutable, and false signals count against the signaller organ's witnessed record, the way organ reputation already works in the Iris architecture. (c) **Typed and scoped:** organ · failure class · severity · scope; an untyped alarm is noise, and noise is how organs learn to ignore pain. (d) **Latching and rate-limited:** repeat signals do not multiply; the state persists quietly until it changes (the supervisor's own CAP discipline is the primitive instance — signal once, then silence with state). (e) **Protective-only authority:** a damage signal may trigger holds, refusals, preservation — never permissions. No signal can authorise a repair, a spend, or an unholding; repair remains a separate authorised act, exactly as the examiner's pathway states. (f) **The dead-detector problem:** the signaller's silence is itself monitored — a heartbeat whose absence is the signal (this is, in fact, how the Kubo failure was actually caught for 100 boots: absence, logged). Every property above is DEFINED from estate practice; as a system, nociception remains NOT DEMONSTRATED.
