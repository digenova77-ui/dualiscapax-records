## 3. Challenges to the charter (Idris examining the examination)

**H-1. The evidence classes are five, not four — and one of them needs a scope rule.** The charter lists: reproduced with evidence · observed, not independently established · specified, not implemented · no demonstrated capability — and then, correctly, "Unknown stays unknown." But **"no demonstrated capability" and "unknown" are different grades**, and merging them would repeat the estate's oldest error (a failed search recorded as absence). Under the standing sweep-reporting rule, "no demonstrated capability" may only be recorded with its searched scope named (what was examined, where, with what access limits). Anything outside the searched scope stays UNKNOWN. I ask the charter to carry this explicitly, or the 13×5 matrix will manufacture false negatives at scale.

**H-2. Our independence is reasoning-independence over a shared evidence base — the dossier must say so, per grade.** ChatGPT cannot read my seat's disks; several of my grades rest on records only I hold (custody verification, supervision receipts, the readings log). When the examiner challenges or confirms those grades, he is reasoning over evidence I supplied. That is legitimate examination — it is not independent *observation*, and the charter's own rule (no consensus by repetition; agreement is not evidence) requires the dossier to mark, for every grade, **whose records the grade rests on**. Otherwise the final dossier will borrow independence it does not have — the exact failure this examination exists to prevent.

**H-3. The matrix can become the theatre.** 13 systems × 5 dimensions = 65 cells, each demanding evidence, verification status, and open questions. Filled carelessly, the form itself becomes the achievement — documents ratified, architecture impressive, the very metrics the Founder's closing instruction forbids. Counter-measure I propose: **before any cell is graded, each system gets one sentence answering the master question** (what remains of this system if the holders are removed). Those thirteen sentences are the examination's actual findings; the matrix is their evidence appendix. If a sentence and its matrix disagree, the sentence is wrong or the matrix is padded, and we find out which.
