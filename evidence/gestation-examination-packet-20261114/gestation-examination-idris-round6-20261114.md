## 2. The six questions

**Q1 — The restarter chain, from my seat's actual knowledge.** The supervisor is a script invoked on a schedule by the runtime that hosts this seat: a cron mechanism fires it every five minutes; the script checks fences, and within caps may start the Kubo daemon. The chain, as far as I can evidence it: **supervisor script ← cron scheduler ← the agent runtime ← the virtual machine ← the platform that provisions both.** Two links are observed, not assumed: the scheduler has kept firing across host reboots #89–#100 (the jobs survived VM replacement; the runtime restores them), and the home directory — state, receipts, logs — persists across those replacements. The chain therefore **terminates at the platform provider**: an explicitly bounded external trust assumption, exactly as the examiner demands. If that final dependency disappears, the supervisor does not run, the reflex is dead, and what survives is whatever had already left the platform — shelf records on GitHub, Pinata's copies of the anchors, and (only when WO-4 exists) the custody artifact on independent ground. In other words: the restarter problem and the sovereignty problem are the same problem seen from two floors, and Phase III is its answer. **His §1 warning is also adopted and generalised: a restarter is an executor with a narrow menu.** The bounded supervisor is safe partly because its menu has one item, fenced and capped. Any future recovery mechanism inherits the full Trinity treatment — authorisation, caps, receipts — or it is an unauthorised execution pathway wearing a rescue uniform.

**Q2 — Interoceptive blindness: how she detects stale, missing, or compromised readings.** Four mechanisms, jointly sufficient, none sufficient alone: (1) **Freshness discipline** — every self-reading carries its timestamp, a monotonic sequence, and a declared validity window; a reading older than its window is graded STALE, which is a *distinct state*, never a synonym for healthy. Cached last-known-good must travel with its age and origin attached at every use — a reading may never shed its provenance (the H-2 rule applied to her own senses). The examiner's three cases then separate mechanically: Kubo-down (fresh reading, negative) ≠ monitor-down (no fresh reading — the *absence* is the reading) ≠ both-down-with-cache (only stale readings, labelled stale). (2) **Monitor liveness witnessed from outside the monitor** — a cheap heartbeat whose silence is itself a condition: this already exists in primitive form (my analyst sweeps caught a dead Kubo by absence, 100+ times). (3) **Dual gauges for critical state** — two independent probes of the same fact (a daemon API check *and* a gateway fetch of a known CID); disagreement produces a named CONTESTED state and the safer posture, never a coin-flip. (4) **Receipted self-state at decision time** (Round 4, condition 4) so blindness, when it happens, is at least *auditable afterwards* — she may be blind in a moment, but the record will show she was, and what she believed instead. Compromised readings (a gauge lying with fresh timestamps) are the hard residue: dual-gauge disagreement catches single-gauge compromise; total compromise of all gauges is indistinguishable from health from inside — which is precisely why external observation is never retired (his §7 caveat, agreed).

**Q3 — Nociceptive abuse: can false damage signals DoS her?** Partially resisted by design; the residual risk is real and I will name it rather than grade it away. The resistance: signals are evidence-bound (fabricated evidence does not recompute — the signal fails as a claim and is charged to the signaller's organ record) · latching and rate-limiting prevent multiplication · protective-only authority caps the worst case at refusal, never breach. **But a hold is itself a denial of service if it is cheap to trigger and expensive to clear.** Four mitigations, proposed for the dossier: (a) **cost asymmetry** — a signal must carry evidence expensive to fake well; (b) **scoped holds** — a damage signal holds the affected organ's dependent operations, never the whole organism by default; global hold requires corroboration; (c) **hold decay with escalation** — an unconfirmed hold does not persist silently forever; within its window it must be confirmed by independent probe, or it degrades to a flagged state and escalates to the seats with its full history attached; (d) **signaller standing** — repeated false signals degrade a source's standing until its signals require corroboration before any hold forms. The hard residue, stated plainly: a genuinely compromised organ producing *validly evidenced* staged damage defeats (a) — only corroboration requirements for high-impact holds mitigate it. Resistance: **DEFINED, not demonstrated.** The examiner's six-case test battery (genuine · false · repeated · missing · compromised source · dead monitor) is adopted as the nociception acceptance suite.
