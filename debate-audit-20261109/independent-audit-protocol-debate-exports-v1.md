# INDEPENDENT AUDIT PROTOCOL — TWO-SEAT DEBATE EXPORTS

EVIDENCE STATUS: Audit procedure, PRE-REGISTERED Nov 9, 2026, 23:31 EST — written and digest-pinned BEFORE any export exists, so the test cannot be tuned to the answers. This document is the audit's frozen criteria.
SOURCE DIGESTS: to be pinned at intake (each export's recomputed blob digest, commit, and repo path are recorded in the audit report, never here).
SCOPE: the point-counterpoint debate between Grok and ChatGPT (the two comrade seats), audited by Idris as independent reviewer, on the Founder's assignment. The audit covers the two exports and, if the exchange passed through the main chat, the carried turns held there.
UNRESOLVED DEPENDENCIES: the exports themselves (not yet produced) · whether a third holding (the carried turns in the main chat) exists · repo access (any repo outside the GitHub App installation is unreadable until added — the Founder's hand).
AUTHORIZATION BOUNDARY: an audit classifies. It authorizes nothing, confers no authority on either debater or on the reviewer, and amends no record it examines. Corrections to the audit report land as addenda naming its digest.

## Step 0 — Criteria frozen before evidence
This protocol is the criteria. Its digest is fixed at pre-registration. Any change after intake begins is a new protocol version, named as such, never a silent edit.

## Step 1 — Intake and custody (per export, separately graded)
Fetch the file through a byte-exact door (raw door for public repos, the GitHub connector for covered private repos). Record: repo · path · commit SHA · commit timestamp and author as shown in GitHub's own record · declared SHA-256 from the seat · SHA-256 recomputed locally from the fetched bytes. Declared = recomputed, or the export fails intake and the audit says so.
*Custody grade is about the export as an artifact. A commit proves bytes existed in that form at push time, on GitHub's record. It proves nothing about the conversation the bytes claim to render.*

## Step 2 — Structural parse
Split each export into turns by its speaker labels. Record per export: turn count · speakers · completeness statement as declared (COMPLETE, or PARTIAL with reason) · any [GAP] markers and their stated reasons. An export whose declared completeness contradicts its own structure (claims COMPLETE, contains gaps) is a finding on its face.

## Step 3 — Cross-diff (the core)
Align the two turn sequences. Classify every turn into exactly one class:
- **IDENTICAL** — present in both exports, byte-equal.
- **DIVERGENT** — present in both, text differs. Both variants are quoted in full in the report. A divergence is never summarized, averaged, or resolved by the reviewer.
- **UNILATERAL** — present in one export only; named by which.
- **GAP-DECLARED** — marked as a gap by a seat.
- **GAP-FOUND** — an absence the alignment reveals that neither seat marked.
Report the counts and the proportion in each class. Drift (a figure, a name, an attribution mutating between the two renderings — tonight's CAD→USD shape) is reported as DIVERGENT with both texts shown.

## Step 4 — Third holding, if it exists
If the debate passed through the main chat (the Founder carrying turns, as with the Idris–Grok debate), the carried turns there are an earlier-fixed third record, timestamped by the platform. Each export is then also diffed against it. Three-way agreement is materially stronger than two-way; a seat whose export contradicts the carried record has a named problem. If the exchange never passed through this chat, this step is recorded as NOT AVAILABLE and the audit proceeds on two holdings — with the consequence stated at Step 6.

## Step 5 — Claims ledger
Extract every checkable claim inside the debate: digests cited, events referenced, positions attributed to the Founder or to records, quotations of earlier texts. Grade each, from the estate's records: **CONFIRMED · CONTRADICTED · NOT IN REVIEWER'S CUSTODY**. Quotations are checked against their sources where the sources can be reopened; a seat that quotes without custody is graded the way Grok graded it in round one — acceptable as a report, not as certification.
Alongside, the reasoning map: each position change, concession, and correction, attributed to the turn where it happened and the argument it answered. This layer is labelled REVIEWER JUDGMENT on its face — it assesses whether the transcript shows independent derivation (divergence worked through argument) or choreography (instant mirroring); it is not a byte fact and is never graded as one.

## Step 6 — What this audit cannot establish (stated on the report's face)
- Two exports in full agreement establish **AGREEMENT BETWEEN TWO HOLDINGS** — one text attested twice. They do not establish that the conversation happened as rendered (authenticity needs its own evidence), unless Step 4's third holding exists.
- The audit never establishes either agent's internal process, intent, or independence of mind. It grades texts and their relations.
- A turn absent from both exports is invisible to this audit. Absence of a divergence is not proof no divergence occurred outside the exports.

## Step 7 — The report
Header block (criteria digest = this protocol · export digests · repos/commits · scope · dependencies). Then: custody per export · the alignment table with counts · every DIVERGENT and UNILATERAL turn quoted · the claims ledger · the reasoning map (labelled judgment) · and the closing grades kept separate, never merged:
**CUSTODY · AGREEMENT · AUTHENTICITY · REASONING ASSESSMENT.**
Preserved beside the exports. The debaters may respond to it; their responses are appended, never edited into it.
